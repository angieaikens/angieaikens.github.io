About

I started my career close to the work, implementing systems, solving customer problems, and seeing firsthand where process, technology, and people collide.

Over time, the scope got bigger: partner ecosystems, engineering organizations, executive operations, enterprise programs, and organizational systems.

What stayed consistent was the part I was drawn to: figuring out what wasn’t quite working yet, creating structure around it, and helping people move forward together.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of business operations, leadership, organizational effectiveness, and complex program execution.

I partner with mission-driven organizations to bring clarity to complexity, strengthen systems, and enable teams to do their best work.

What keeps me curious

I’m curious about how things fit together, whether it’s spices in a recipe or decisions in the office.

Why do some combinations work effortlessly while others create friction? What changes when one small piece moves? And why can something that looks complicated sometimes become surprisingly simple once you understand how the parts relate?

That curiosity shows up everywhere for me. I like understanding how things work, finding the connection that isn’t immediately obvious, and figuring out what might make them work better.

What I value

I believe in giving back to the communities I’m part of with my time, financial support, board service, and mentoring.

I’m especially drawn to work that supports women and children and helps address hunger. I believe strong communities are something we build together, and that each of us has an opportunity to leave the places we live a little stronger, healthier, and better than we found them.

For me, success includes contributing to that.
