Case Study 4

Building a Workforce
Operating System

Workforce Strategy  •  Business Operations  •  Process Transformation

01   The Challenge

A new 10,000+ person organization had significant hiring demand, but the operating infrastructure was still being built.

Requests, approvals, recruiting activity, budgets, and workforce planning crossed multiple teams without one consistent system connecting them.

Hiring demand was moving faster than the operating model around it.

The need was bigger than request tracking. Leaders needed a repeatable way to connect people, money, capacity, and decisions from request through employee start.

02   What I Built

I helped build the end-to-end operating model for hiring and workforce decisions.

The process standardized intake, approvals, recruiting visibility, reporting, and decision support across the full request-to-start funnel.

I partnered across Talent Acquisition, Finance, Workforce Planning, and business leadership while managing more than 2,500 hiring requests across a $30M+ resource budget.

03   How I Used Data

The model turned hiring into an operating decision, not just an open requisition.

Capacity planning and hiring economics helped leaders understand what a request meant for budget, delivery capacity, contractor strategy, and the rest of the hiring pipeline.

That included approved headcount, salary versus budget, RSU spend, contractor reduction, geographic cost differences, time to hire, and the cost of leaving critical work unfilled.

The point was not that one answer was always better. The operating model made the tradeoff visible so leaders could choose based on skills, urgency, location, budget, and organizational capacity.

04   How the Model Scaled

1

A dedicated inbox and spreadsheets created the first workable intake and tracking process.

2

Standardize

Requests, approvals, status, and reporting were brought into a repeatable operating rhythm.

3

Inform

Recurring reporting surfaced demand, funnel movement, budget position, risk, and decisions needed.

4

Digitize

The process matured into a structured Oracle APEX application instead of a collection of manual trackers.

5

Scale

The broader organization, including newly acquired Cerner managers, was onboarded to the operating model.
