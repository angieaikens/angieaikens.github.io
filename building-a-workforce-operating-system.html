Case Study 1

Building a Workforce Operating System

Workforce Strategy • Business Operations • Process Transformation

Oracle Health was standing up a new Product & Engineering organization with ambitious growth plans but almost none of the operating infrastructure needed to manage hiring at that scale. I was tasked with creating the hiring process and budget tracker.

2,500+Hiring requests managed
$30M+Resource budget
~12,000Person organization at launch
01   The Starting Point

The challenge was not fixing a broken process. There was no process.

Executives had growth priorities and approval authority. Finance had budgets. Talent Acquisition had recruiting work underway. Hiring managers had roles to fill.

What was missing was the operating layer connecting those pieces so leaders could manage the hiring portfolio as a whole.

RequestApprovalBudgetRecruitingTradeoffsStart

One leadership view across the hiring portfolio.

02   What I Built

I designed the hiring operating model from the ground up.

I defined how requests entered the system, what information managers had to provide, how approvals and budget were captured, how requests moved through the process, and what leaders needed to see.

Talent Acquisition owned recruiting. I connected that work to approvals, budget position, pipeline status, expected starts, and leadership decisions.

Built into the operating model
  • Standardized intake and approval tracking
  • Budget and capacity logic
  • Recurring leadership reporting
  • Manager onboarding and decision rules
03   How It Scaled

The model grew with the organization.

The first workable version used approval documents, a dedicated inbox, spreadsheets, and clear decision rules. As volume grew, I strengthened the intake, reporting, budget controls, and manager onboarding.

When the manual model became too error-prone, I translated it into requirements for an Oracle APEX application. The organization later transitioned to a stronger enterprise APEX model developed within OCI.

Build→Structure→Automate→Transition
Business Impact

I was asked to build a hiring process and budget tracker. What the organization needed was an operating system.
