Enterprise Governance & Regulatory Compliance

Building What Didn’t Exist Yet

During an acquisition, a compliance tracking tool disappeared overnight while the obligation remained. I designed and built the governance operating model and reporting infrastructure needed to restore accountability across multiple Lines of Business.

1The Challenge

The system disappeared.
The obligation did not.

A tool used to track regulatory compliance disappeared overnight during an acquisition. Employees moved from a single-threaded leader into multiple Lines of Business, creating fragmentation, risk, and operational overload.

The organization needed a new way to assign ownership, track obligations, report status, and demonstrate control.

Fragmented obligationsRequirements spread across teams and documents
Missing toolCompliance tracking disappeared during the acquisition
Multiple Lines of BusinessDifferent processes and interpretations
Accountability riskOwnership needed to be re-established clearly
Increased risk without a shared operating model
2What Didn't Exist Yet

A governance model built from scratch.

I designed and built a unified compliance operating model and reporting tool from the ground up in six months. The new approach created a shared structure for ownership, tracking, reporting, and review across multiple Lines of Business and an employee population in the tens of thousands.

Unified Governance Model
Define & AssignClear accountability for regulatory obligations
TrackCentralized obligations and commitments
ReportAccurate, timely leadership and regulator reporting
ReviewConsistent validation, testing, and attestation

From missing system to durable model.

Define ownership. Rebuild the tracking process. Create consistent reporting. Establish a review cycle that could hold up across multiple Lines of Business.
