Case Study

Building What Didn’t Exist Yet

Enterprise Governance • Regulatory Compliance • Large-Scale Integration

A sudden tool loss during an acquisition became the catalyst for a new compliance operating model and reporting tool.

The Problem

A tool used to track regulatory compliance disappeared overnight during an acquisition.

At the same time, employees who had previously operated under a single-threaded leader were redistributed across multiple Lines of Business inside a much larger company.

What I Built

I designed and built a unified compliance operating model and reporting tool from the ground up in six months.

The new approach created a shared structure for ownership, tracking, reporting, and review across multiple Lines of Business and an employee population in the tens of thousands.
