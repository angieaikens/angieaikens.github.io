Case Study 2

Building What Didn’t Exist Yet

Enterprise Governance • Regulatory Compliance • Large-Scale Integration

During an acquisition, a compliance tracking tool disappeared overnight while the obligation remained. I designed and built the governance operating model and reporting infrastructure needed to restore accountability across multiple Lines of Business.

6 monthsBuilt from the ground up
20K+Employees supported
0Audit review findings
01   The Problem

The system disappeared. The obligation did not.

Employees moved from a single-threaded leader into multiple Lines of Business, creating fragmentation, risk, and operational overload.

The organization needed a new way to assign ownership, track obligations, report status, and demonstrate control.

Unified Compliance Operating Model
DefineTrackReportReview
02   What I Built

I created a unified compliance operating model and reporting tool.

The model established shared accountability and a common structure for regulatory work across independently led organizations.

It gave leaders one way to see ownership, commitments, status, and review activity instead of relying on disconnected local practices.

Built from the ground up
  • Clear accountability across Lines of Business
  • Centralized obligation tracking
  • Leadership and regulator reporting
  • Consistent review and attestation
03   Business Outcome

The new model held up under scrutiny.

The governance model scaled across a population in the tens of thousands and supported consistent review and reporting across multiple Lines of Business.

The result was a durable operating structure with zero audit review findings.

Business Impact

A missing tool became an opportunity to build a stronger governance system than the organization had before.
