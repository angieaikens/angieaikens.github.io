Case Study 2

Giving Leadership Time Back

Leadership Operating Rhythm • Executive Alignment • Organizational Effectiveness

Leadership was spending too much time coordinating work and not enough time moving it forward. I redesigned the operating rhythm to create clearer priorities, ownership, and decision points.

Powered by   Retrospectives   •   Backlog Management   •   Leadership Cadence   •   RACI   •   Release Versioning
01

The Problem

Leadership was spending too much time in meetings, updates, and follow-up instead of making decisions.

Across five leadership teams, operating practices had become fragmented and the rhythm of work was creating friction.

02

What I Changed

I redesigned the leadership operating cadence using retrospectives, backlog management, RACI accountability, and release versioning.

The new rhythm made priorities, ownership, decisions, and follow-through easier to see.

03
