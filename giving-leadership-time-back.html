Case Study

Giving Leadership Time Back

Leadership Operations • Organizational Effectiveness • 1,000-Person Engineering Organization

Redesigned the leadership operating cadence to reduce meeting time, increase clarity, and accelerate decision-making.

The Problem

Leadership was spending too much time coordinating work and not enough time moving it forward.

Across five leadership teams, operating practices had become fragmented. Meetings, follow-up, accountability, and prioritization were consuming time without consistently creating clarity.

What I Changed

People were busy. Leadership was engaged. The operating rhythm was getting in the way.

I assessed maturity across five leadership teams, identified burnout drivers, and redesigned the leadership operating cadence using retrospectives, backlog management, RACI accountability, and release versioning.
