Case Study 3

Giving Leadership Time Back

Leadership Operating Rhythm • Executive Alignment • Organizational Effectiveness

Across a 1,000-person engineering organization, fragmented leadership practices were consuming time and slowing decisions. I redesigned the leadership operating cadence to make priorities, ownership, and decision-making clearer across five leadership teams.

33%Less leadership meeting time
5Leadership teams
1,000Person engineering organization
01   The Problem

Leadership was spending too much time coordinating and not enough time deciding.

Across five leadership teams, operating practices had become fragmented. Updates, follow-up, and duplicated coordination were consuming time without consistently improving decisions.

BeforeMore meetingsDuplicated coordinationUnclear ownership
→
AfterFocused forumsClear decisionsVisible ownership
02   What I Changed

I redesigned the leadership operating cadence.

I replaced open-ended coordination with focused, topic-based forums and clearer decision points.

Retrospectives, backlog management, RACI accountability, and release versioning made priorities, ownership, and follow-through easier to see.

New operating rhythm
  • Retrospectives
  • Backlog management
  • RACI accountability
  • Release versioning
03   Business Outcome

The new rhythm gave leadership time back.

The organization reduced leadership meeting time by 33% while improving executive readiness and decision velocity.

The goal was not fewer meetings for its own sake. It was a better operating rhythm for the work.

33%less meeting time5leadership teams
Business Impact

The best leadership cadence is not the one with the most meetings. It is the one that makes the right decisions easier to make and easier to follow through.
