Case Study 3

Rapid Response, Fewer Escalations

Customer Response • Process Design • Operational Infrastructure

High-stakes customer issues were too dependent on people knowing who to call, what to check, and when to escalate. I built a step-by-step response guide with the right contacts, troubleshooting steps, and escalation paths.

Outcome: Step-by-Step Response Guide
1. Identify

Capture the issue and understand the impact.

2. Right Contacts

Connect with the people needed to move quickly.

3. Troubleshoot

Follow structured steps to isolate or resolve the issue.

4. Escalate

Use defined paths when additional support is needed.

5. Resolve / Close

Drive the issue to closure using clear criteria.

The Problem

High-stakes customer issues were too dependent on people knowing who to call, what to check, and when to escalate.

Response quality depended too much on individual experience rather than a repeatable operating path.

02

What I Built

Built operating infrastructure from the ground up for high-stakes customer situations, including a step-by-step response guide that gave teams the right contacts, troubleshooting steps, and escalation paths.

The framework created a repeatable path from issue identification through closure.
