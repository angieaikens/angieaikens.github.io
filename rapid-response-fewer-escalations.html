Case Study

Rapid Response, Fewer Escalations

Client Experience • Incident Management • Process Design

Built operating infrastructure from the ground up for high-stakes customer situations.

The Problem

High-stakes customer issues were too dependent on people knowing who to call, what to check, and when to escalate.

That made response quality too dependent on individual experience rather than a repeatable operating path.

What I Built

Built operating infrastructure from the ground up for high-stakes customer situations, including a step-by-step response guide that gave teams the right contacts, troubleshooting steps, and escalation paths.

The goal was simple: give teams a clear path from first alert through resolution without relying on tribal knowledge.
